Carry an Ars Nouveau warp scroll and its destination shows up as a JourneyMap waypoint. Put the scroll away and the waypoint disappears.

Both the Warp Scroll and the Stabilized Warp Scroll count, from your main inventory, hotbar and offhand. A scroll renamed on an anvil gives the waypoint that name. Several scrolls pointing at the same spot make one waypoint. Scrolls in chests or ender chests are not read, and warp portals are not shown.

Only the client needs this mod: it reads the scrolls in your own inventory. Requires [Ars Nouveau](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/ars-nouveau) and [JourneyMap](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/journeymap).
